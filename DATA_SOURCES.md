# Data sources and attribution

This repository contains processed analytical outputs derived from multiple secondary datasets used in the accompanying manuscript.

## Main source categories

- Agricultural statistics: BPS Kabupaten Barru
- Tourism attractions and points of interest: OpenStreetMap and relevant local tourism sources
- Road-network data: OpenStreetMap
- Vegetation information: Sentinel-2
- Elevation and terrain information: NASADEM
- Land-cover information: ESA WorldCover
- Destination-level visitor records: Dinas Pariwisata Kabupaten Barru

Detailed dataset descriptions, temporal coverage, analytical functions, and citations are provided in Supplementary Table S1 of the manuscript.

## Administrative visitor records

The original destination-level visitor workbooks for 2021–2025 are not
distributed through this repository. Access remains subject to the conditions
of Dinas Pariwisata Kabupaten Barru. The 2023–2025 observations define the
primary demand window. The 2021–2022 observations are used only in the
fixed-sample temporal-window sensitivity analysis because they represent
pandemic and early-recovery conditions and have less consistent monthly
reporting.

## Processed Hybrid AECS grid

`data/processed/agrotourism_corridor_grid_result_HYBRID.geojson` is the processed analytical grid used for the manuscript's post-grid analyses.

Users should consult the original data providers for applicable licences, terms of use, and attribution requirements.
